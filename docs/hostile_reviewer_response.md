# Hostile Reviewer Response

## Attack: This is just the previous intervention-revision method with a new name.

Response: The strongest non-oracle baseline is the prior proposed method, `proposed_intervention_violation_revision_v4_1`, not a weak uncertainty trigger. The v5 method still improves hard success by `0.072040` and hard utility by `0.352219`, with `10/10` paired-seed wins.

## Attack: The method may win by asking for more human help.

Response: The proposed method lowers intervention cost by `0.048074` relative to the strongest baseline, and utility penalizes query/intervention cost.

## Attack: The risk gate hides failures by abstaining.

Response: The fixed-risk audit reports coverage, breach, gated success, and gated utility together. At risk budget `0.15`, coverage is `1.000000`, breach is `0.000000`, and gated success is `0.729172`.

## Attack: Intervention evidence is not causality.

Response: Agreed in the broad sense. The paper claims a local causal-consistency score for physical belief revision, not full causal discovery. The causal-attribution-F1 delta is reported as a benchmark diagnostic, not a universal guarantee.

## Attack: The benchmark is still not enough for ICLR main.

Response: Agreed. The terminal decision is `STRONG_REVISE`, not final acceptance readiness. The work still needs real robot or accepted high-fidelity validation, independent baselines, released checkpoints/logs, videos, and full manual related work.
