# Literature Map

## Crowded Areas

- Interactive VLA manipulation.
- Action-conditioned world models.
- Intervention-centric causal representation learning.
- Belief-space safety filters.
- Failure-aware RL and human-in-the-world-model post-training.

## Local Novelty Boundary

The paper should claim intervention-gated physical belief revision for robot recovery. It should not claim a new VLA architecture, a new human-querying interface, or a universal belief-space safety filter.

## Evidence Needed For Final Main-Conference Readiness

- Transfer to an external benchmark or high-fidelity simulator.
- Real robot intervention logs.
- Manual related-work synthesis against accepted interactive manipulation and belief-space baselines.
- Stronger ablations tying each violated-physical-assumption label to downstream recovery choices.
