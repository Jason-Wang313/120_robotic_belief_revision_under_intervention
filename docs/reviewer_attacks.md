# Reviewer Attacks

- The evidence is synthetic and may not transfer to real hardware.
- The prior proposed intervention-revision method is already strong, so v5 must show that causal intervention gating adds something beyond tuning.
- Fixed-risk gating may hide hard cases through abstention.
- Human interventions can be false, malicious, semantic, or operator-specific.
- Causal consistency does not prove full causal discovery.
- The literature boundary may be too close to belief revision, POMDPs, shared autonomy, robot learning from correction, and safety filters.

Response: the paper emphasizes physical-belief scope, fixed-risk coverage and breach, ablations, explicit failure conditions, and honest local scope. It should not be submitted as ICLR-main ready until external validation, hardware/high-fidelity logs, checkpoints, videos, independent baselines, and full manual related work are available.
