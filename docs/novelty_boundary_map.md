# Novelty Boundary Map

## What This Paper Can Claim

- Causal intervention-gated physical belief revision reduces false revisions, missed violations, unsafe revisions, and damage under local intervention stress.
- Physical-violation hypotheses, counterfactual consistency, belief-delta memory, query-cost modeling, fixed-risk gating, calibration, operator guards, and recovery value all contribute locally.
- The local benchmark supports this claim against no revision, Bayesian update, uncertainty trigger, ensemble disagreement, conformal filtering, human-intervention revision, POMDP-style update, active-inference query, causal-discovery revision, and the prior proposed method.

## What This Paper Cannot Claim

- Real-world robot deployment readiness.
- State-of-the-art belief revision or shared autonomy.
- A new low-level controller.
- A universal causal-discovery theory.
- Hardware safety.

## Boundary Sentence

This work studies when intervention evidence is sufficient to revise action-critical physical robot beliefs under a fixed revision-risk budget; it does not propose a new robot controller, a universal POMDP solver, or hardware-validated safety.
